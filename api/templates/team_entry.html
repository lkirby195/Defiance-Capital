Overview

Deal Economics

A SPLIT_DRAW or SPLIT_PRINCIPAL loan is advanced in two parts, so both portions are required and have to add up to the loan requested (SPEC §8.2). A NO_DRAW or WHOLETAIL loan is one advance and carries neither. The four boxes marked with a default are the config stand-in when left blank; the interest rate has none and the ledger cannot be laid out without it.

Valuation, rent and analyses (SPEC §6.1 — until the adapters land)

Both toggles blank take the default the §3 exit implies: flip on for a resale, rental on for a hold or whenever a rent is entered.
