Deal Economics

One term, said either way: enter a term in months or a payoff date and the other derives — the payoff date is the closing date plus the term, to the day (SPEC §8.1). A payoff date may be any date after closing: the term is the whole months that fit inside it and the days left over are a stub, which the ledger prices as a short final period (SPEC §8.3). Enter both boxes and they have to agree, which means a term in months lands on its own month.

A Split Draw or Split Principal loan is advanced in two parts, so both portions are required and have to add up to the Loan Amount (SPEC §8.2). A No Draw or Wholetail loan is one advance and carries neither. The four boxes tagged default are pre-filled from config and editable; leave one alone and the deal follows config, type over it and the deal carries your number. Holding costs are a percentage of the price plus the rehab and the dollars it comes to are shown under the box. The interest rate has no default and the ledger cannot be laid out without it.

Valuation, rent and analyses (SPEC §6.1 — until the adapters land)

The estimated sale price is what LTV is computed on (SPEC §7.4) and what the Flip analysis sells at. Without it the screen goes Conditional and the flip is not evaluated; nothing else about the deal depends on it. Without a monthly rent the Rental and Take-Back analyses are not evaluated — nothing stands in for what a property lets for. Each toggle on Default takes what the §3 exit implies: flip on for a resale, rental on for a hold or whenever a rent is entered. The Take-Back analysis has no toggle; it runs on every deal (SPEC §8.6).
